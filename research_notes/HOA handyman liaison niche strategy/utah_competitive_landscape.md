# Utah Wasatch Front: Competitive Landscape for Handyman / Small-Repair Services Sold to HOAs and Property Managers (as of Oct 2026)

**Method note (read first):** Direct page fetches were blocked in this research environment (DNS failures via WebFetch; proxy returned 403 on curl), so every finding below comes from web-search result snippets of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Figures are therefore "as snippeted"; confidence levels reflect that. Pages that could not be opened for verification are marked where relevant. Data is 2024-2026 unless labeled older.

## KQ1: Which handyman / property-maintenance companies in the Salt Lake-Provo metro explicitly market to HOAs or property managers, and how?

### Takeaway
Only one handyman brand in the metro has a dedicated HOA page: Mr. Handyman of Lehi, Provo & Spanish Fork (4.9 stars / 365 Google reviews), which pitches a "single provider so boards don't coordinate multiple contractors" model focused on exterior work. Everyone else is either a residential handyman that mentions "property managers" in passing (Su Casa, Valley Brothers, Handy Pro, HoneyDo), an exterior/landscape contractor branding itself "HOA Specialists" (OneSource), or a national management company's maintenance arm (Associa "Professional Maintenance," FirstService maintenance staff, CCMC onsite teams). No company found in the metro positions itself as a neutral coordinator/dispatcher of vetted independent handymen with a work guarantee.

## KQ2: Do Utah HOA management companies run in-house maintenance divisions?

### Takeaway
Yes for the largest locals and nationals (FCS Community Management has 12 maintenance staff and a GC license; Associa, FirstService and CCMC field maintenance/facilities staff; Park City's All Seasons does basic repairs in-house), but the evidence for mid-size Wasatch Front managers (Advantage, Welch Randall, Treo, HOA Strategies, PMI Reliant) points to vendor coordination rather than crews. Property Management Systems could not be found.

- FCS (approx. 200 communities, GC license, 12 maintenance staff) is the only Utah-headquartered manager that is clearly a direct competitor for small-repair work inside its own portfolio; but 12 technicians across ~200 communities (~17 communities per tech) implies overflow demand even there.

- Park City managers (All Seasons, Model HOA, Sea to Ski) are a distinct resort-condo market; not the Wasatch Front focus but show that in-house basic repair is a pattern among condo-heavy portfolios.

## KQ3: How do Utah CAMs find vendors (CAI Utah trade show, directories, software marketplaces, Facebook, referrals)?

The Utah CAI chapter (branded UCCAI) runs an annual Northern Utah Vendor Trade Show (2026: April 30, Viridian Event Center, West Jordan; 2025 booths $500 and sold out) plus a Southern Utah reverse trade show ($300 manager booths), and the chapter has roughly 320 members. Software-side, Vantaca's "Vantaca Vendor" automates vendor onboarding/compliance/invoicing for 550+ management companies, making W-9 and COI readiness a gating factor. No Utah HOA-manager Facebook group or CINC vendor directory could be confirmed.

### Inferences

- Software is becoming a gatekeeper: a liaison that arrives "Vantaca-ready" (W-9, COI, license on file; clean per-work-order invoices) removes the payment-hold friction that Vantaca's defaults create for individual handymen.
- Referrals and Facebook likely matter but are unverified for Utah; treat as a hypothesis to test by interviewing managers.

## KQ4: Handyman pricing in the metro (hourly, minimums, trip charges, HOA vs residential)

### Takeaway
Published local price points cluster at $57-$70/hour for independents and marketplaces (TaskRabbit ~$57/hr SLC; Upkeep Lehi $70/hr; Lee's House Doctor $65/hr after a $160 two-hour block), with Thumbtack minimums of $60-$100 and flat small-job rates of $75-$150; franchise rates are nationally $75-$125/hr but Mr. Handyman Utah quotes by the job and publishes no rates. No Utah source publishes a commercial/HOA rate differential or a trip charge.

### Cited Findings
- Thumbtack (Provo/Lehi/Sandy pages show identical generic guidance): minimum rates typically $60-$100; flat-rate examples — faucet replacement $125, toilet replacement $75, ceiling fan/light fixture $75, garbage disposal $75, attic ladder $150; handymen may charge flat fees for predictable jobs and hourly for larger ones — [Thumbtack Provo](https://www.thumbtack.com/ut/provo/handyman); [Thumbtack Sandy](https://www.thumbtack.com/ut/sandy/handyman). Confidence: high (not city-specific).
- HomeYou estimates average handyman cost in Lehi at $352-$525 per job, range $92-$930 — [HomeYou Lehi](https://www.homeyou.com/ut/handyman-services-lehi-costs). Confidence: low (aggregator model, not survey).
- TaskRabbit estimates ~$57/hour for handyman Taskers in Salt Lake City — [TaskRabbit SLC general handyman](https://www.taskrabbit.com/locations/salt-lake-city/general-handyman). Confidence: medium.
- Upkeep Home Services (Lehi) lists handyman labor at $70/hour inside its subscription — [Upkeep Lehi](https://upkeephomeservices.com/lehi-home-maintenance/). Confidence: medium-high.
- Lee's House Doctor (Farmington) block pricing for two-person crews: 2 hours $160, 4 hours $280, then $65/hour — [Porch listing](https://pro.porch.com/farmington-ut/handymen/lees-house-doctor/pp). Confidence: medium.
- Mr. Handyman of Utah: "straightforward pricing by the job, not the hour"; no dollar figures — [Mr. Handyman Utah](https://www.mrhandyman.com/locations/utah/). Confidence: high.
- National benchmarks: Housecall Pro says self-employed handymen charge $50-$80/hr and corporate/franchise handymen $75-$125/hr — [Housecall Pro pricing guide](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/how-to-price-handyman-jobs/); Instaservice (2026) says most homeowners pay $45-$125/hr — [Instaservice 2026 cost](https://www.instaservice.com/blog/?p=38300). Confidence: medium.
- Pay to labor (not customer price): ZipRecruiter SLC handyman job posting $30-$40/hr (W-2) — [ZipRecruiter Handyman](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Handyman); Lula (property-manager platform) 1099 handyman/plumbing repair role in SLC listed at $40-$90/hr (2022, dated) — [Lula posting via Paylocity](https://recruiting.paylocity.com/Recruiting/Jobs/Details/3718628). Confidence: medium (Lula data is 2022).
- Associa OnCall offers "hourly and contracted rates" to associations (national, 2019) — [Associa hub](https://hub.associaonline.com/u-s/associa-hires-jamie-luke-as-senior-vice-president-of-associa-oncall). Confidence: medium.

### Inferences
- A liaison can plausibly bill HOAs $85-$110/hr (franchise-equivalent) while paying vetted 1099 handymen $40-$60/hr, leaving a 35-50% gross margin to fund coordination, insurance and the guarantee — this is an inference from the spread between TaskRabbit/independent rates (~$57-$70) and franchise rates ($75-$125) and 1099 pay ($40-$90), not a cited figure.
- Mr. Handyman's refusal to publish hourly rates is a messaging opening: a transparent, published HOA rate card (hourly + trip fee + minimum) would be differentiated locally.

### Gaps
- No Utah-specific trip charge, service-call minimum, or after-hours multiplier was found for any firm.
- No source documents HOA/commercial vs residential rate differences in Utah.
- Mr. Handyman Utah franchise half-day/full-day package prices not found.

## KQ5: Labor supply — how many handymen/maintenance workers, what they earn, how easy to recruit 1099 handymen

### Takeaway
BLS-derived data (via WageDex, May 2025) puts ~6,590 general maintenance and repair workers in Salt Lake City-Murray (median $52,650/yr, ~$25.31/hr) and ~2,500 in Provo-Orem-Lehi (median $48,990), with 15,790 statewide (May 2024, median $49,070). Utah unemployment was 3.5% in August 2026 (vs 4.1% US), but the state's chief economist says hiring has "cooled around the edges," and marketplaces show deep informal supply (1,000+ TaskRabbit Taskers; 30-40 Thumbtack pros per city), so 1099 recruiting should be feasible at $40-$60/hr.

### Cited Findings
- Salt Lake City-Murray MSA, SOC 49-9071 (May 2025 BLS OEWS via WageDex): median $52,650/yr (~$25.31/hr), ~6,590 jobs — [WageDex SLC-Murray](https://wagedex.com/jobs/maintenance-and-repair-workers-general/metro/salt-lake-city-murray-ut). Confidence: medium (third-party repackaging of BLS; could not open bls.gov).
- Provo-Orem-Lehi MSA (May 2025 via WageDex): median $48,990/yr, ~2,500 jobs — [WageDex Provo-Orem-Lehi](https://wagedex.com/jobs/maintenance-and-repair-workers-general/metro/provo-orem-lehi-ut). Confidence: medium.
- Utah statewide (May 2024 via WageDex): median $49,070/yr ($23.59/hr), ~15,790 jobs — [WageDex Utah](https://wagedex.com/jobs/maintenance-and-repair-workers-general/utah). Confidence: medium.
- National BLS: median annual wage $48,620 (May 2024) — [BLS OOH General Maintenance and Repair Workers](https://www.bls.gov/OOH/installation-maintenance-and-repair/general-maintenance-and-repair-workers.htm); May 2023 OES: median $46,700, mean $49,650, ~1.5M employed, hourly 10th pct $14.84 to 90th pct $35.33 — [BLS OES May 2023 49-9071](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/May/oes499071.htm). Confidence: high.
- Utah unemployment: 3.5% seasonally adjusted in August 2026 (down 0.1 from July), ~64,100 unemployed, 1,788,800 jobs; US 4.1%; chief economist Ben Crabb said the hiring landscape "has cooled around the edges," tightening competition for part-time and entry-level work — [Utah Business / DWS August 2026 summary](https://www.utahbusiness.com/press-releases/2026/09/18/utah-employment-summary-august-2026-increased-economy-20600-jobs/). Rate was 3.8% Feb-Apr 2026, 3.7% May, 3.6% June (~66,200 unemployed) — [DWS June 2026 rate PDF](https://jobs.utah.gov/wi/update/press/pdfs/ratejun26.pdf); [DWS April 2026 rate PDF](https://jobs.utah.gov/wi/update/press/pdfs/rateapr26.pdf). Confidence: high.
- Informal supply: TaskRabbit 1,000+ handyman Taskers in SLC area — [TaskRabbit SLC](https://taskrabbit.com/locations/salt-lake-city/home-improvement); Thumbtack ~30 handymen Lehi, ~40 The Avenues, ~22 Kaysville — [Thumbtack Lehi](https://www.thumbtack.com/ut/lehi/handyman); [Thumbtack Avenues](https://www.thumbtack.com/ut/the-avenues/handyman). Confidence: medium.
- Active recruiting signals: Mr. Handyman of Salt Lake and Wasatch Counties hiring a handyman in Pleasant Grove; inSync Staffing listing SLC handyman roles for a modular-building company — [Monster Utah handyman jobs](https://www.monster.com/jobs/q-handyman-jobs-l-utah?page=9); Salt Lake Brewing Company seeking a 1099 handyman contractor for a multi-month engagement covering preventive maintenance and work orders — [City Weekly jobs](https://jobs.cityweekly.net/job/11b7c17f-339e-4d6d-a277-85841a0a9f23/plumber-needed-salt-lake-city); Lula 1099 property-maintenance handyman $40-$90/hr (2022) — [Lula posting](https://recruiting.paylocity.com/Recruiting/Jobs/Details/3718628). Confidence: medium.
- Payscale maintains a Salt Lake City hourly page for Maintenance and Repair Worker, General (figure not captured in snippet) — [Payscale SLC](https://www.payscale.com/research/US/Job=Maintenance_and_Repair_Worker%2C_General/Hourly_Rate/79396939/Salt-Lake-City-UT). Confidence: n/a.

### Inferences
- Roughly 9,000 employed general maintenance workers across the two MSAs, plus 1,000+ gig handymen on TaskRabbit alone, indicates ample 1099 supply; the liaison's challenge is quality/vetting, not headcount.
- A $45-$60/hr 1099 offer sits well above the W-2 median (~$25/hr) and in the middle of what marketplaces pay, so it should attract experienced W-2 maintenance techs moonlighting or going independent, especially as hiring "cools around the edges."

### Gaps
- Official BLS OEWS metro tables for Salt Lake City and Provo-Orem could not be opened (bls.gov unreachable in this environment); WageDex figures are unverified repackaging.
- No count of licensed handyman/"S-" specialty contractors from Utah DOPL; no Craigslist posting-volume data; no Indeed listing counts (Indeed did not surface).
- No Utah-specific data on 1099 vs W-2 classification enforcement for handymen.

## KQ6: Messaging benchmarks — competitor/national HOA-maintenance language, clichés to avoid, and what managers complain about

### Takeaway
Dominant HOA-maintenance language is "partner" and "one provider" copy ("Your Partner in Community Success," "One partner for every common area," "single point-of-contact account manager," "bonded and insured," "on-call after-hours"). The documented pain points are small jobs being ignored by contractors, vendor no-shows, communication gaps causing missed appointments and incomplete work, paperwork friction (COI/W-9 payment holds), and manager burnout from workload (71% moderate-to-severe). Utah-specific vendor complaints were thin; the state conversation is about rising HOA costs and disputes, not vendor quality.

### Cited Findings

**Competitor / national copy (clichés to avoid or subvert)**
- "Your Partner in Community Success" — Associa Utah page title — [Associa Utah](https://click.associaonline.com/csshoa/).
- "One partner for every common area" (sidewalks, mailboxes, pool decks) — [In and Out Property Care HOA page](https://inandoutpropertycare.lovable.app/services/hoa-community-maintenance).
- "Consolidating services with one provider at a community creates efficiencies in terms of scheduling, management and billing" — [MCS blog](https://blog.mcs360.com/hoa-property-service-providers-make-communities-shine); MCS also publishes a "HOA services you should be outsourcing" checklist — [MCS checklist PDF](https://commercial.mcs360.com/hubfs/CHECKLIST-THE-HOA-SERVICES-YOU-SHOULD-BE-OUTSOURCING.pdf).
- "Single point-of-contact account manager" for HOAs and master-planned communities — [IronCrest HOA services](https://ironcrest.catdi.com/services/hoa-community-services).
- "RealMaintenance" with on-call, after-hours emergency services — [RealManage HOA maintenance](https://www.realmanage.com/hoa-maintenance-services); Sentry Management launched a maintenance program in Nashville — [Sentry Management](https://sentrymgt.com/nashville-expands-services-with-new-maintenance-program).
- Mr. Handyman: "blends the personalized service of a locally owned business with the reliability of a national franchise network"; "pricing by the job, not the hour" — [Mr. Handyman Utah](https://www.mrhandyman.com/locations/utah/); HOA pages stress "bonded and insured," GL + workers' comp, vendor agreements, "work through a queue" — [Mr. Handyman Vero Beach HOA](https://www.mrhandyman.com/vero-beach-jupiter/commercial-services/hoa-property-managers/); [Mr. Handyman Mooresville HOA](https://mrhandyman.com/mooresville-lake-norman-concord/commercial-services/hoa-property-maintenance).
- PMI Reliant (Orem): "24-hour on-call maintenance," "vendor insurance tracking," "bid coordination" — [PMI Reliant Lehi](https://www.orempropertymanagementinc.com/lehi-association-management).
- Local independents: "one call" (Trusted Handyman Utah, HoneyDo), "within 24 hours" (Handy Pro), "scheduled within 2-3 days" (HoneyDo), "licensed and insured" (all) — [Trusted Handyman](https://trustedhandymanutah.com/); [HoneyDo](https://honeydohome.com/); [Handy Pro](https://handyproutah.com/).
Confidence on all copy: medium (snippets).

**Pain points / complaints**
- Most firms prefer larger jobs because they pay better and "contractor" carries more status than "handyman"; advice to homeowners is to bundle small repairs or piggyback onto larger jobs — [HomeAdvisor: Small Home Repairs, Who Should You Call](https://homeadvisor.com/r/?p=10515); [HomeAdvisor Q&A](https://homeadvisor.com/r/?p=5822). Confidence: medium.
- A homeowner forum thread describes an HOA with "a strong history of vendors just not showing up" while the HOA threatened fines for missed access — [Bob Is The Oil Guy forum](https://bobistheoilguy.com/forums/threads/fighting-hoa-anyone-have-any-luck.306413/). Confidence: low (single anecdote, location unknown).
- "Vendor communication gaps lead to missed appointments, incomplete work, and contractor frustration" — [HOA Living blog, July 2026](https://www.hoaliving.com/blog/2026/07/15/when-to-hire-a-community-management-company/). Confidence: medium (HOA Living is FCS's brand, so this is a Utah manager's own framing).
- Minnesota legislative testimony from a longtime board member alleged management companies favor particular vendors and discriminate against small-business vendors, raising costs — [MN CIC/HOA working group testimony](https://lcc.mn.gov/cichoa/meetings/20241206/Kathleen-Phillips-testimony). Confidence: medium (not Utah).
- 71% of community managers report moderate-to-severe burnout; 80% have thought about leaving; drivers are difficult homeowners, board conflict and heavy workload (FCAR, July 2026, n=287) — [ManageCasa summary](https://managecasa.com/articles/hoa-manager-burnout-retention). Confidence: medium.
- Vantaca's default vendor payment hold reasons — "Pending Insurance Documentation," "Awaiting W-9" — show the paperwork friction small vendors create — [Vantaca support](https://support.vantaca.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025047351-How-to-Set-Up-or-Edit-a-Service-Provider). Confidence: high.
- Utah context: the Utah HOA ombudsman gets about one request per day and launched a working group on HOA issues and rising costs — [KSL](https://www.ksl.com:443/article/news/features/family/utah-hoa-ombudsman-launches-working-group-to-tackle-issues-and-rising-costs/51496526); the ombudsman issues nonbinding advisory opinions — [TownLift](https://townlift.com/2025/09/utah-unveils-new-office-to-help-with-hoa-disputes/). An r/SaltLakeCity homeowner reported a surprise $1,400 HOA invoice for cleaning/sanitizing/pest control after the HOA had only announced an extermination visit — [The Cool Down](https://www.thecooldown.com/green-home/unexpected-hoa-fee-bill-homeowner/). FSR Utah has published "11 Critical Questions to Ask About Your HOA's Maintenance" (2018, older) — [FSR Utah article](https://www.fsresidential.com/Utah/News-Events/Articles/11-Critical-Questions-to-Ask-About-Your-HOA’s-Main). Confidence: medium.
- Connecticut's consumer agency lists poor communication, slow/no response and lack of transparency as the most common HOA-management complaints (not Utah) — [CT DCP](https://portal.ct.gov/dcp/knowledge-base/articles/consumer-hub/condos/most-common-hoa-complaints). Confidence: medium.

### Inferences
- Clichés to avoid: "partner," "one-stop," "trusted," "licensed and insured" as a headline, "we treat your community like our own." Every competitor uses them; none are differentiating.
- Gaps to exploit in copy: (1) the small-job problem ("contractors won't come out for a $200 fix") is documented and un-owned locally; (2) the manager's paperwork burden (COI, W-9, per-work-order invoices that match the software) is a felt pain that no handyman markets to; (3) accountability — a guarantee that the *coordinator* owns the outcome, not the individual handyman; (4) transparency — published rates vs. Mr. Handyman's quote-only model; (5) manager burnout — messaging about taking work-order chase-up off the manager's plate rather than "maintenance quality."
- Utah managers' public discourse (ombudsman, cost pressure) suggests "predictable, itemized small-repair cost" is a timely message for boards worried about rising dues.

### Gaps
- No Utah-specific manager reviews or forum posts complaining about handyman vendors by name were found; Reddit r/HOA threads did not surface via search.
- No survey quantifying vendor no-show rates or COI compliance rates.
- Could not read full competitor HOA pages, so exact taglines beyond snippets are unverified.

## KQ7: Growth context — HOA formation and new master-planned communities on the Wasatch Front

### Takeaway
Utah's rebuilt state registry showed 3,364 registrations (367 master HOAs, 2,997 sub-HOAs) as of October 2025, versus CAI's estimate of ~3,800 community associations; over 58% of Utah home listings in 2025 carried HOA fees, and high-density product (condos/townhomes) is now a large share of permits, with three northern Utah County cities taking 18% of statewide permits in 2024. Daybreak (CCMC), The Point (Draper) and Utah City (Vineyard) are adding thousands of HOA-governed units. Year-over-year new-HOA formation counts were not published.

### Cited Findings
- Utah Commerce presentation (Oct 2025): "Total registrations: 3364," "Total Master HOA's: 367," "Total Sub-HOA's: 2997"; top cities by registration are Salt Lake City, Park City and St. George — [Utah Public Notice presentation PDF](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/1486081.pdf). Confidence: medium-high (could not open PDF; snippet).
- CAI 2025 Statistical Review lists Utah at 3,800 community associations (condos, co-ops, planned communities) — [CAI Foundation 2025 Statistical Review](https://foundation.caionline.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025StatisticalReviewFoundation.pdf). Confidence: medium-high.
- More than 58% of new Utah listings in 2025 included HOA fees (Realtor.com data via Axios, Mar 2026) — [Axios SLC: HOAs gain ground](https://www.axios.com/local/salt-lake-city/2026/03/30/utah-hoa-market-share-dues-increasing); nearly half of Salt Lake listings required HOA fees in 2024 — [Axios SLC 2025](https://www.axios.com/local/salt-lake-city/2025/03/12/salt-lake-hoa-fees-home-listings?stream=top). Confidence: high.
- Registry rebuild: new system opened Sept 2025; old system discontinued after Oct 31, 2025; all associations had to re-register by Nov 7, 2025; $90 fee; annual renewal; non-registered HOAs cannot impose or enforce liens — [Commerce re-registration notice](https://commerce.utah.gov/2025/09/12/all-utah-hoas-must-re-register-now-in-the-new-state-system/); [Commerce registration page](https://commerce.utah.gov/hoa/new-registration/); public search — [HOA registry search](https://commerce.utah.gov/hoa-registry-search/). - Daybreak (South Jordan): managed by CCMC; base HOA fee $144.50/month for 2026 (includes internet); townhomes/condos pay an additional sub-association fee covering snow pushing, yard care and some exterior maintenance; board includes Larry H. Miller Real Estate and residents; The Pennant, a 190-unit multifamily building, opens Fall 2026 in Downtown Daybreak — [Daybreak HOA page](https://www.daybreakutah.com/whats-happening/daybreak-residents-2/daybreak-hoa); [Daybreak future of living](https://www.daybreakutah.com/whats-happening/a-healthly-plan/from-vision-to-reality-the-future-of-daybreak-living/). Confidence: high.

- Kem C. Salt Lake and Utah Counties each took ~24% of all Utah permits 1994-2024 — [Salt Lake Tribune, Jan 2025](https://sltrib.com/news/2025/01/14/how-fast-is-your-town-growing). Confidence: medium-high.

### Inferences
- The 2,997 sub-HOAs (vs 367 masters) are the sweet spot: small sub-associations in Daybreak-style master plans have exterior-maintenance obligations (snow, yard, "some exterior maintenance") but no onsite staff, so they generate exactly the fragmented small-repair work a liaison aggregates.
- Utah County's permit share (Eagle Mountain, Saratoga Springs, Lehi) plus Utah City and The Point imply continued new sub-HOA formation through the late 2020s; developer-controlled boards and their managers (CCMC, FSR, FCS) are early-entry accounts.
- The mandatory annual registry (with public search) is a free, current prospect list of every Utah HOA with contact info — a practical lead source the report should highlight.

- No HOA/CC&R details found for The Point or Utah City.
- No 2025 county-level townhome/condo permit split for Salt Lake vs Utah County.
